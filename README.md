# 1. **[Bicycle And Equipment Sales Analysis](BicycleAndEquipment_SalesAnalysis)**

## Project Overview

The **Business Sales Analysis** project focuses on evaluating sales and business performance through the analysis of transactional and commercial data.

The objective of the project is to identify the main drivers of sales performance, determine which products and business areas contribute the most to revenue and profitability, and identify areas where business performance can be improved.

### Main Analytical Areas

- Sales and revenue performance
- Product performance
- Profitability analysis
- Business performance trends
- Customer purchasing activity
- Identification of high- and low-performing areas
- Business recommendations based on analytical findings

### Business Objective

The analysis is designed to answer questions such as:

- Which products and business segments generate the strongest results?
- Where are the main sources of revenue and profit?
- Which areas are underperforming?
- What factors may explain changes in business performance?
- Which areas should receive additional attention or resources?

# 2. **[Customer Behavior Analysis](ClothingStore_CustomerBehavior_Business Analysis)**

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer behavior, purchasing patterns, profitability drivers, and loyalty metrics for a clothing retail business operating in the United States.

The analysis covers customer segmentation, product performance, shipping preferences, subscription effectiveness, geographic performance, discounts, and seasonal purchasing behavior. Based on these findings, strategic recommendations are developed to improve revenue, customer retention, and operational efficiency.


#  Analytical Approach

The portfolio projects are structured around a business-oriented analytical workflow:

```text
Business Question
       ↓
Data Collection
       ↓
Data Cleaning & Validation
       ↓
Exploratory Data Analysis
       ↓
Segmentation & KPI Analysis
       ↓
Trend & Performance Analysis
       ↓
Business Insights
       ↓
Recommendations
```

The key principle is to move beyond describing the data and connect analytical findings with potential business actions.
