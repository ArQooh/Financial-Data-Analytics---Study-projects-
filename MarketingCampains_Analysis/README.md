# Campaign Performance Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes the performance of advertising campaigns across META, TikTok, and dv360 platforms during the period from June 2026 to October 2026.

The analysis focuses on campaign performance, budget allocation, clicks, impressions, conversions, CTR, and the effectiveness of different advertising formats.

## Key Findings

1. **META generated the most conversions** within the `ProductLaunch_Jesen2026` campaigns.

2. **The largest share of the budget was allocated to the `ProductLaunch_Jesen2026` campaigns.**  
   Total spending reached **€258,640**, with **€148,000** spent on the dv360 platform.

3. **dv360 generated the highest number of clicks and impressions** for the `ProductLaunch_Fall2026` campaign, accounting for **33% of total clicks**.

4. **The `BrandAwareness_Summer2026` campaign had a critically low number of conversions**, with only **23 conversions**, despite having a higher CTR than the more successful `ProductLaunch_Fall2026` campaign.

5. **September was the most effective and, at the same time, the most expensive month** in terms of advertising costs during the period from June 2026 to October 2026.

6. **Reels on META generated the highest number of clicks and conversions**, accounting for **17% of total clicks** and **34% of total conversions**.

7. **Spark_Ads was the most effective advertising format on TikTok.**

8. **The higher performance of `ProductLaunch_Fall2026` compared with `BrandAwareness_Summer2026` was likely influenced by higher advertising investment on the dv360 platform.**  
   This additional investment may have contributed to the higher number of conversions generated on META and TikTok. However, the quality and effectiveness of the advertising campaign itself may also have influenced the results.

## Conclusion

The analysis indicates that **higher advertising investment, platform selection, and advertising format can have a significant impact on campaign performance**.

`ProductLaunch_Fall2026` demonstrated stronger conversion performance compared with `BrandAwareness_Summer2026`, while META and dv360 played an important role in generating conversions, clicks, and impressions.

Further optimization should focus on reallocating budget toward the most effective platforms and advertising formats while investigating the reasons behind the low conversion rate of the `BrandAwareness_Summer2026` campaign.
