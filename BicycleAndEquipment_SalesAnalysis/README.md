# Key Findings

## Geographic Performance

**Top countries by Sales Volume**
1. United States  
2. Australia  
3. United Kingdom  

**Top countries by Revenue**
1. United States  
2. Australia  
3. United Kingdom  

**Additional insights**
- **Canada** and the **United Kingdom** show the strongest profitability metrics.
- **Australia** has the lowest profitability indicators for bicycles among all countries.
- **France** demonstrates below-average product ROI compared to other regions.

---

## Product Category Performance

### Top category by revenue contribution:
**Road Bicycles and Mountain Bicycles** — accounting for approximately **64%** of total profit.
### Top category by sales volume:
**Accessories** (primarily tires & tubes, bottles & cages, and helmets).
### Top category by return on investment (ROI):
**Accessories** — consistently high profitability and strong margin performance.

---

## Temporal Trends
- **Sales peaked in 2015**, driven primarily by a **50% increase** in bicycle sales, with an average order value of approximately €850.

- In 2016, **overall profit declined by 10%**.
Primary cause: an 18% drop in bicycle category sales.

# Recommendations

| Recommendation | Rationale |
|----------------|-----------|
| Address the 2016 profit decline through targeted marketing and supply-chain review | 18% drop in bicycle sales drove a 10% overall profit reduction. ROI levels support promotional campaigns and selective price reductions of 10–15%.|
| Expand sales efforts in Canada | Highest ROI performance relative to other markets. |
| Increase bicycle sales in Canada and the United Kingdom | These markets show the strongest profitability and return metrics. |
| Review and adjust bicycle pricing strategy in Australia | Lowest profitability indicators among all countries analyzed. |
| Continue supporting growth of the **Accessories** category | High ROI + solid profit contribution |
| Reassess pricing policy in **France** | Below-average product returns vs other regions |

---

## Conclusion

Road and mountain bicycles remain the primary profit drivers of the business, while the **Accessories** category delivers the highest return on investment.

Geographic performance varies significantly — **Canada** and the **United Kingdom** offer the strongest opportunities for margin expansion.

The sharp decline in bicycle sales in 2016 requires immediate attention through:
- Targeted marketing activation  
- Pricing flexibility  
- Supply-chain optimization  

Implementation of the recommendations above is expected to restore growth momentum and improve overall business efficiency.
